ABOUT THE STUDIO

Small parts.
A style of your own.

RAMIBLE Studio / 라미블 스튜디오

RAMIBLE Studio is an independent game studio based in South Korea. We make games that connect the fun of choosing with the feel of playing.

Our first game, Rivet Rumble, brings robot building and action together. We’re shaping an experience where a different combination leads to a different way to play—and a reason to try again.

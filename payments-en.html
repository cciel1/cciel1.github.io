Rivet Rumble purchase and refund support

If purchased gems are missing or you need help with a purchase or refund, use the steps below. Rivet Rumble is preparing for release. This website does not sell items or process payments.

Email purchase and refund support

Missing purchased gems

Restart the game, check that you are using the same game account as when you purchased, and use the purchase-history check. A pending purchase may not have granted gems yet. If the problem continues, contact support before buying the same item again.

What to include

Send your nickname, product, purchase time, app version, device and a description of the issue. Include the Google Play order ID (starting with GPA), if available. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, card details, bank authentication information or full purchase tokens.

Request a Google Play refund

Use the Google Play refund process or contact our support email. Google’s general guidance distinguishes requests within 48 hours, which depend on purchase details, from later requests to the developer. This distinction does not remove statutory withdrawal, defect, non-performance or minor-protection rights. See the Google Play refund policy.

Refunds and game currency

Use of gems or a box is not a blanket reason to refuse every refund. We review the delivery method, usage and applicable rights. A confirmed refund or cancellation may reverse or adjust the corresponding gem grant. Contact support if you disagree with an adjustment.

When testing purchases

Joining an internal or closed test does not make every purchase free. For a test purchase, check that the Google Play purchase sheet explicitly shows a test purchase and test payment instrument. Do not proceed for testing if a real payment method or charge is shown.

Account deletion and refunds are separate

Uninstalling the app or deleting a game account does not automatically refund a purchase. See account and data deletion for deletion requests.

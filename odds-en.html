Part and assembly odds

Development snapshot, not the final app. Check the notice in your installed version before purchasing or assembling. This page does not announce a public game release.

Effective date: 2026-11-04

1. What a box holds

One random part box holds one each of Armor panel, Servo motor, Cooling core, Compute cartridge, Sensor module, Power cell: 6 parts. The slots are fixed; only each part's maker (one of 10) is random.

2. Maker odds per slot

For every slot each of the 10 makers has the same 10% chance. Each slot sums to 100%.

3. Notes

  • The chance of a specific part (slot + maker) within its slot is 10%. Each part is drawn independently; earlier parts do not affect later ones.
  • A 10-box product is the same as opening one box that many times. Bundles do not change the odds.
  • There is no pity or guarantee: buying more does not guarantee any specific part.
  • The odds do not change with purchase count, time, account or owned parts. If they ever change, this table and its effective date change first.
  • A maker box (1/10 boxes) holds one part of each of the 6 slots from the maker you pick; nothing in it is random. The daily maker box also grants parts from the maker displayed before purchase.

The numbers and names on this page are generated from the same data the game server uses to open boxes.

Robot assembly

Six selected parts fix two model candidates under the current recipe rule. You choose one; the model and its grade are not rolled again. Repeating the same recipe gives the same two candidates. Starter-part-only recipes exclude legendary models.

HP, Attack, Speed, Skill haste are drawn separately. Each displayed integer from 0 to 10 has probability 1/11 (9.0909091%) per field. One exact four-value combination has probability 1/14641 (0.0068301346%). The total is not uniformly distributed.

Robot disassembly

An eligible disassembly returns 2 parts. Each draw independently selects one of 60 part types: 1/60 (1.6666667%). Duplicates are possible; the consumed recipe does not change these odds.

Rewarded ad parts

For an eligible, server-verified reward, the part count is 1, 2, 3, each 1/3 (33.333333%). Slots are selected uniformly without replacement from six slots, and each selected part has a uniformly drawn maker. No gems are awarded. Official sample ads do not grant game items.

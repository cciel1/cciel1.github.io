Delete your Rivet Rumble account and data

You can request deletion of your Rivet Rumble account and related data without reinstalling the app. RAMIBLE Studio receives the request and verifies account ownership before processing it.

Request account deletion by email

Request without the app

Email ramible.dev@gmail.com. Use “Rivet Rumble account deletion request” as the subject and include your nickname and sign-in method (guest, username or linked account). Send from an address where you can receive our reply. We explain ownership verification and the result. A matching email address alone does not prove ownership or authorize deletion.

Request in the app

Sign in to the relevant account, open account management and choose account deletion. Reauthenticate using the current password, fresh linked-provider authentication or the original guest-device credential. Button locations and available sign-in methods may vary by app version.

Data deleted

After ownership verification, deletion removes login, session and account-link information, nickname, friendships, robots, parts, gems and game progress from the live server. Progress cannot be restored, so check the account carefully. Uninstalling or signing out does not delete the server account.

Records that may remain

Recovery backups expire within 30 days and are not used to restore a deleted account. Minimal deletion markers and pseudonymized shared battle results remain separate from game progress for the lifetime of the service. If transaction evidence or other records must be retained separately, we explain the categories, reasons and retention periods in the response.

Guest accounts and verification

The original device with its guest credentials provides the clearest ownership check. If a device change or app-data deletion has removed those credentials, a nickname alone may not identify the owner. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, full purchase tokens, card details, identity documents or government identifiers by email.

Refunds and linked accounts

Game-account deletion is separate from a Google Play refund. It does not delete your Google or Kakao account. You may need to revoke the external app connection separately in the provider’s settings. Use the same support email for deletion-status or privacy questions.
